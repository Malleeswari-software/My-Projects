Welcome to Loyola
Model High School

Loyola Model High School (Montessori Wing) brings together the essential elements of education in a way that preserves the spirit of learning and creates a positive, lifelong impact on children.

Established in 1979 in Vanasthalipuram, Hyderabad, Loyola is permanently recognized by the Government of Telangana. We nurture academic excellence alongside scientific temper, free enquiry, individual talent, character and values—helping every child grow with confidence and purpose.

Academic Progression

Learning Beyond the Classroom

A thoughtful progression that builds foundations, curiosity, capability and readiness.

01

Pre-Primary (Montessori)

Nursery, PP1 & PP2. Play-way foundation learning through sensory exploration, motor skill activities, phonics, and socialization.

02

Primary (Classes I – V)

CBSE curriculum. Strong academic foundations, communicative English, numeracy, inquiry, and experiential activity stations.

03

Middle School (Classes VI – VIII)

CBSE curriculum. Conceptual deep dives, science laboratories, computer programming, project analysis, and independent research.

04

High School (Classes IX – X)

Telangana State Board SSC curriculum. Rigorous board examination coaching, past-paper analysis, leadership, and 100% distinction focus.
